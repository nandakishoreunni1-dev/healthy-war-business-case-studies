
# Healthy War — Sales Decline Analysis

## 1. Business Problem

A company is experiencing a decline in sales. One possible reason is poor inventory management.

Popular and high-demand products are unavailable in the warehouse. As a result, customer demand is not being satisfied, and competitors may be capturing those sales opportunities.

## 2. Business Objective

Identify the reasons behind declining sales and recommend practical improvements to inventory management and customer satisfaction.

## 3. Initial Hypothesis

Poor inventory management may be contributing to the sales decline because:

- High-demand products are unavailable.
- Customer needs are not being fulfilled.
- Competitors may be gaining customers.
- Lost sales opportunities may be affecting business performance.

## 4. Data Required

Before confirming the root cause, the following information is needed:

- Monthly sales data.
- Product-wise demand and sales.
- Inventory availability and stockout records.
- Customer complaints and feedback.
- Competitor product availability.
- Supplier delivery and replenishment times.

## 5. Preliminary Recommendations

1. Identify high-demand products.
2. Monitor inventory levels regularly.
3. Improve demand forecasting.
4. Review supplier delivery performance.
5. Prioritise replenishment of frequently unavailable products.

## 6. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Product stockout rate.
- Inventory turnover ratio.
- Monthly sales growth.
- Order fulfilment rate.
- Customer satisfaction.

## 7. Expected Business Impact

Improved inventory availability may help the company fulfil customer demand, reduce lost sales opportunities and improve customer satisfaction.

## 8. Conclusion

Inventory management is a possible contributor to the company's sales decline. Further data analysis is necessary to confirm the root cause and evaluate the effectiveness of the recommended solutions.
